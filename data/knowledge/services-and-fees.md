# Services and Fees

## Services we offer

We provide general and family dentistry: exams, cleanings, X-rays, fillings, crowns, root canals, extractions, night guards and teeth whitening. We see adults and children aged 3 and up.

## Services we do not offer

We do not place braces or clear aligners, and we do not perform oral surgery under general anesthesia. For these we refer patients to specialists nearby.

## Standard fees

These are our fees before insurance. A new patient exam with X-rays is $149. A routine adult cleaning is $119. A child cleaning is $89. Professional teeth whitening is $349. Fees for fillings, crowns and other treatment depend on the tooth and are quoted after an exam.

## Appointment length

Appointments booked online are 30 minutes long, which covers a cleaning, a check-up or a first exam. Longer treatment visits are scheduled by the front desk after your exam.
