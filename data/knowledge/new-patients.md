# New Patients

## Becoming a patient

We are accepting new patients. You can book your first visit online with the assistant or by calling the front desk. No referral is needed.

## What to bring

Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, a list of medications you take, and any recent dental X-rays. If your X-rays are at another office, ask them to send the files to records@maplegrovedental.example.

## Forms

New patient forms are emailed to you after you book. Please complete them before you arrive, or come 15 minutes early to fill them in at the office.

## Your first visit

Your first visit includes a full exam, X-rays if needed, and a conversation with the dentist about any concerns. If time allows, a cleaning is done at the same visit; otherwise it is booked as a follow-up.

## Children

A parent or legal guardian must attend with patients under 18 and sign their forms.
