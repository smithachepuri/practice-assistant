# Insurance and Payment

## Insurance plans we accept

We are in-network with Cascade Dental PPO, Northstar Dental and Summit Health Dental. We can also file claims for most other PPO plans as an out-of-network provider. We do not accept HMO or DMO plans.

## Checking your coverage

Bring your insurance card to your first visit. We verify your benefits before treatment and give you a written estimate of what your plan is expected to pay and what you will owe. Estimates are not a guarantee of payment by your insurer.

## Patients without insurance

Patients without insurance can join our Maple Membership plan for $29 a month. It covers two cleanings, two exams and one set of X-rays each year, and gives 15% off other treatment.

## Payment methods

We accept cash, debit cards, all major credit cards, and HSA and FSA cards. Payment is due at the time of the visit. We do not accept personal checks.

## Payment plans

For treatment costing more than $500, we offer interest-free payment plans over 3 or 6 months. Ask the front desk to set one up before your treatment begins.
