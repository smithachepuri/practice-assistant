# Hours and Location

Maple Grove Dental is a fictional practice used for this demo.

## Office hours

We are open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The office is closed for lunch from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM. We are closed on Saturdays, Sundays and major public holidays.

## Address and parking

We are at 1200 Maple Grove Avenue, Suite 210, on the second floor. Free parking is available in the lot behind the building, and there are two accessible spaces next to the rear entrance. The building has an elevator.

## Contact

Call the front desk at (555) 010-0142 during office hours. You can also email frontdesk@maplegrovedental.example. We reply to email within one business day.

## Languages

Our team speaks English and Spanish. Tell us when you book if you would like an interpreter for another language and we will arrange one.
