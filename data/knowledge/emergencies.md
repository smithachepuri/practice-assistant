# Dental Emergencies

## When to call 911

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room for trouble breathing or swallowing, bleeding that will not stop, swelling that is spreading to the eye or neck, or a serious injury to the face or jaw.

## Urgent dental problems

For a severe toothache, a knocked-out or broken tooth, or a lost crown, call our emergency line at (555) 010-0199. The line is answered 24 hours a day and a dentist will call you back.

## Same-day visits

We hold a small number of same-day appointments for urgent problems on weekdays. Call the front desk as early in the day as possible to ask for one.

## What the assistant can do

The online assistant can book a regular appointment but cannot assess symptoms or give medical advice. For anything urgent, please call.
