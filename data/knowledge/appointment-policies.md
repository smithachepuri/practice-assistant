# Appointment Policies

## Cancelling or rescheduling

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. You can do this with the assistant using your confirmation code and the phone number you booked with, or by calling the front desk.

## Late cancellation and missed appointments

A cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice, or a missed appointment, carries a $50 fee. The fee is waived once per calendar year, and always waived for illness or an emergency.

## Running late

If you arrive more than 15 minutes late we may need to reschedule your visit so that other patients are not kept waiting. Call the front desk if you are delayed.

## Reminders

We send a text reminder two days before your appointment and again on the morning of your visit. Reply STOP to opt out of text reminders.

## Booking ahead

Appointments can be booked up to 30 days in advance.
